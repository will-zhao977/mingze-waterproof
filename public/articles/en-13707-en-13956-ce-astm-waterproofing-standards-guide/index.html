Importing Waterproofing Membranes from China: Understanding EN 13707, EN 13956 (CE) & ASTM Standards

Technical Summary for Specification & Procurement

Manufactured by Weifang Mingze Waterproof Material Co., Ltd., export-grade waterproofing membranes comply with European harmonized standards (EN 13707 for flexible bitumen sheets, EN 13956 for thermoplastic TPO/PVC single-ply sheets) and American standards (ASTM D6164, ASTM D6878, ASTM D4434, ASTM D5385), verified by third-party testing (SGS, TÜV Rheinland) with full Factory Production Control (FPC) and Declaration of Performance (DoP) documentation for customs clearance.

1. What Are the Critical Differences Between European (EN), American (ASTM), and Chinese (GB) Waterproofing Standards?

Direct Engineering Answer: While European EN standards (such as EN 13707 and EN 13956) prioritize harmonized Declaration of Performance (DoP), factory production control (FPC), and declared threshold values (MLV/MDV) for CE marking, ASTM standards define rigid prescriptive physical property classifications (Type I/II/III), and Chinese National Standards (GB 18242, GB 27789, GB 12952, GB/T 23457) combine strict minimum physical thresholds with rigorous factory batch testing.

When civil consulting engineers, general contractors, and commercial importers source bituminous or polymeric waterproofing membranes from China, navigating cross-border compliance requires understanding how test methodologies, specimen conditioning, and certification frameworks diverge:

  1. European Union (CE Marking & CPR 305/2011):
  2. Under the European Construction Products Regulation (CPR (EU) No 305/2011), waterproofing membranes imported into the European Economic Area (EEA) must carry a valid CE Mark backed by an EN 13707 (reinforced bitumen sheets for roof waterproofing) or EN 13956 (plastic and rubber sheets for roof waterproofing) certification.
  3. EN standards evaluate products via System 2+ or System 3 Assessment and Verification of Constancy of Performance (AVCP). Manufacturers must maintain an audited Factory Production Control (FPC) system, issue a formal Declaration of Performance (DoP), and state whether performance attributes represent a Manufacturer's Limiting Value (MLV) or Manufacturer's Declared Value (MDV) with stated statistical tolerances.
  1. American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM):
  2. North American, Latin American, and Middle Eastern tender specifications frequently mandate ASTM D6164 (SBS modified bituminous sheet), ASTM D6878 (thermoplastic polyolefin roof membrane), ASTM D4434 (polyvinyl chloride sheet), or ASTM D5385 (hydrostatic pressure resistance).
  3. Unlike EN standards that allow declared manufacturer thresholds, ASTM specifies strict minimum pass/fail baseline criteria categorized by polymer class, reinforcement type, and sheet thickness grade.
  1. Chinese National Standards (Guobiao - GB / GB/T):
  2. Domestic Chinese standards, including GB 18242-2008 (SBS bitumen), GB 27789-2011 (TPO), GB 12952-2011 (PVC), and GB/T 23457-2017 (Pre-applied HDPE), are among the most demanding globally regarding minimum tensile elongation, heat resistance, and low-temperature cold bend.
  3. High-grade Chinese manufacturers like Weifang Mingze Waterproof Material Co., Ltd. engineer standard production formulations to meet or exceed the intersection of GB Type II, EN CE, and ASTM Type I/II requirements simultaneously.

2. How Do EN 13707 and ASTM D6164 Govern SBS Modified Bitumen Membrane Performance?

Direct Engineering Answer: For SBS modified bitumen membranes, EN 13707 mandates specific European test protocols including EN 1109 (low-temperature flexibility on Ø30mm mandrel), EN 1110 (flow resistance at elevated temperature), and EN 12311-1 (tensile properties), whereas ASTM D6164 classifies sheets into Type I and Type II grades based on minimum polyester reinforcement weight, tensile peak load (≥ 500 to 800 N), and ultimate elongation (≥ 35% to 50%).

Specifiers comparing Weifang Mingze MZ-CAT-01 Elastomeric SBS Bitumen Membrane against project tender documents must cross-reference four critical physical performance dimensions:

  1. Low-Temperature Flexibility (Cold Bend Resistance):
  2. EN 1109 Protocol: Specimens are conditioned at target test temperatures (e.g., -20°C or -25°C) in a refrigerated chamber for 2 hours and bent 180° around a Ø 30 mm polished steel mandrel over 5 seconds. Mingze MZ-CAT-01 Type II exhibits zero surface fissures or delamination at -25°C.
  3. ASTM D5147 Section 12: Mandates testing across a Ø 25 mm (1.0 inch) mandrel bent 180° in 2 seconds. The higher strain rate in ASTM testing requires premium virgin radial SBS block copolymers (12% to 14% polymer modifier content) to prevent brittle micro-cracking.
  1. High-Temperature Flow Resistance (Heat Stability):
  2. EN 1110 Protocol: Suspended vertical specimens undergo a 2-hour exposure in a forced-air convection oven at 90°C, 100°C, or 105°C, measuring downward compound displacement with a micrometer (pass threshold: ≤ 2.0 mm flow).
  3. ASTM D5147 Section 16: Measures flow stability at 102°C (215°F) for 2 hours. Mingze MZ-CAT-01 shows zero dripping or surface sag up to 105°C, ensuring thermal stability on steep slopes in Middle Eastern and tropical installations.
  1. Tensile Strength & Elongation Behavior:
  2. EN 12311-1 (50mm Strip Method): Requires recording peak tensile force ($F_{\text{max}}$) and elongation at break across both longitudinal (MD) and transverse (CMD) orientations. Mingze 4.0mm MZ-CAT-01 (Polyester Mat Reinforcement) delivers ≥ 800 N / 50mm (MD) and ≥ 600 N / 50mm (CMD) with ≥ 40% elongation, accommodating concrete thermal expansion and joint movement.
  3. ASTM D6164 Type I vs Type II: Type I requires ≥ 350 N/50mm (70 lbf/in) tensile; Type II mandates heavy-duty ≥ 500 N/50mm (100 lbf/in) tensile with ≥ 50% elongation.
  1. Dimensional Stability & Hydrostatic Watertightness:
  2. Tested under EN 1107-1 (storage at 80°C for 24 hours; dimensional shrinkage ≤ 0.20%) and EN 1928 Method A / B (watertight at 0.3 MPa hydrostatic pressure for 24 hours).

3. How Do EN 13956, ASTM D6878, and ASTM D4434 Regulate TPO and PVC Single-Ply Membranes?

Direct Engineering Answer: For polymeric single-ply roofing, EN 13956 unifies testing for both TPO and PVC sheets under European methods for dimensional stability (EN 1107-2), tear resistance (EN 12310-2), and foldability at low temperature (EN 495-5), while ASTM D6878 governs TPO with strict requirements for xenon-arc artificial weathering (≥ 10,000 kJ/m²) and heat aging (ASTM D573), and ASTM D4434 governs PVC with tight limits on plasticizer loss (≤ 3.0%) and accelerated water absorption (≤ 3.0%).

Polymer single-ply sheets operate under intense UV irradiance, thermal wind flutter, and mechanical stress. Importers evaluating Weifang Mingze MZ-CAT-08 TPO and MZ-CAT-09 PVC must verify compliance across key durability criteria:

  1. Solar Optical Properties & Aging Verification:
  2. ASTM D6878 (TPO Specific): Mandates that white TPO membranes achieve an initial Solar Reflectance Index (SRI) ≥ 104 (ASTM E1980) and retain mechanical tensile integrity after 10,000 kJ/m² total radiant exposure under ASTM G155 xenon-arc testing without micro-cracking or discoloration. Mingze MZ-CAT-08 passes this benchmark with zero surface crazing under 10x microscopic magnification.
  3. ASTM D4434 (PVC Specific): Evaluates high-molecular plasticizer retention. Under ASTM D1203 Method A, plasticizer extraction loss must remain ≤ 3.0% by weight after oven exposure at 70°C for 24 hours, ensuring the membrane does not prematurely embrittle.
  1. Seam Weldability & Field Joint Peeling Strength:
  2. EN 12316-2 (Peel Resistance of Joints): Measures the mechanical resistance of automated hot-air welded overlaps. Both Mingze MZ-CAT-08 TPO and MZ-CAT-09 PVC yield seam peeling strengths ≥ 800 N / 50mm with cohesive Film Tear Bond (FTB) failure modes.
  3. ASTM D751 (Hot-Air Seam Strength): Requires minimum factory seam shear strength ≥ 80% of parent sheet tensile strength, confirming that properly welded joints are stronger than the un-welded field sheet.
  1. Hydrostatic Pressure & Dynamic Puncture Resistance:
  2. EN 12691 (Resistance to Impact): Evaluates puncture resistance from dropped steel indenters over soft and hard support substrates (≥ 700 mm drop height without leakage).
  3. ASTM D5385 / ASTM D751: Measures static puncture resistance (≥ 250 N for 1.5mm TPO) and hydrostatic water column impermeability (≥ 0.3 MPa for 2 hours).

4. What Laboratory Test Protocols Verify Pre-Applied HDPE Membrane Watertightness (ASTM D5385 & GB/T 23457)?

Direct Engineering Answer: For basement blindside waterproofing, Weifang Mingze MZ-CAT-11 Pre-Applied HDPE Membrane is evaluated under ASTM D5385 and GB/T 23457-2017 (Type P), verifying direct continuous chemical and mechanical bonding to post-poured concrete (peel strength ≥ 2.0 N/mm with 100% concrete cohesion failure) and total resistance to lateral water migration under ≥ 0.7 MPa (70 meters water head) hydrostatic pressure.

Blindside substructure waterproofing requires testing parameters that traditional open-roof standards do not cover:

  1. Adhesion to Poured Concrete (Peel Adhesion Test):
  2. GB/T 23457-2017 & ASTM D903: A test specimen of Mingze MZ-CAT-11 is cast against structural C30 concrete and cured for 28 days. In tensile peeling at 180°, the pressure-sensitive polymer adhesive and particulate coating must bond so aggressively that failure occurs within the concrete mortar phase (cohesive failure ≥ 2.0 N/mm) rather than at the membrane-concrete interface.
  3. Resistance to Lateral Water Migration (Hydrostatic ASTM D5385 Modified):
  4. A concrete specimen cast onto Mingze MZ-CAT-11 is intentionally perforated with a 10 mm central defect and subjected to step-wise hydrostatic water pressure up to 0.7 MPa (7.0 bar / 70m water column). The continuous adhesive seal prevents water from traveling laterally along the interface, restricting moisture strictly to the punctured locus without spreading across the building footprint.
  5. Tensile Properties of Virgin HDPE Carrier Film:
  6. Tested under ASTM D638 / EN ISO 527-3: Virgin HDPE resin sheet (0.8mm to 1.2mm base carrier) achieves tensile yield strength ≥ 22.0 MPa and ultimate elongation ≥ 500%, bridging post-settlement concrete cracks up to 3.0 mm wide.

8. Frequently Asked Questions on International Waterproofing Standards (Substantive Evidence FAQ)

Q1: What is the exact difference between an EN 13707 CE Declaration of Performance (DoP) and an ASTM D6164 test report?

Answer: An EN 13707 CE Declaration of Performance (DoP) is a legally binding regulatory document under European CPR 305/2011 issued by the manufacturer, certifying that the product conforms to declared performance thresholds (MLV/MDV) under continuous Factory Production Control (FPC) audited by an EU Notified Body. In contrast, an ASTM D6164 test report is an empirical physical performance certificate issued by a laboratory verifying that a specific batch meets prescriptive North American physical baselines (such as Type I/II tensile strength and elongation). Weifang Mingze Waterproof Material Co., Ltd. provides both official CE DoP documents and independent ASTM test reports for MZ-CAT-01 SBS Bitumen Membranes.

Q2: Can Weifang Mingze TPO (MZ-CAT-08) and PVC (MZ-CAT-09) membranes satisfy both European EN 13956 and US ASTM requirements simultaneously?

Answer: Yes. Weifang Mingze MZ-CAT-08 TPO (1.5mm Type P) and MZ-CAT-09 PVC (1.5mm) are engineered using premium virgin polymers and high-tenacity polyester scrim reinforcement, enabling them to meet both European EN 13956 (CE marked, ≥ 1100 N/50mm tensile, -40°C cold bend) and American standards (ASTM D6878 for TPO with SRI ≥ 104 and 10,000 kJ/m² xenon weathering; ASTM D4434 for PVC with UL 790 Class A fire rating and ≤ 3.0% plasticizer loss). This dual-compliance simplifies specification for international EPC contractors managing multi-regional projects.

Q3: What mandatory quality documents must an importer request from a Chinese waterproofing manufacturer before dispatching a container?

Answer: Before shipping waterproofing membranes from China, an importer must secure: 1) The official Mill Test Certificate (MTC/COA) from a CNAS-accredited factory laboratory showing batch-specific physical test values; 2) The formal Declaration of Performance (DoP) with CE product labeling for European destinations; 3) An audited ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System Certificate; 4) An independent third-party test report (SGS, Intertek, or TÜV Rheinland); and 5) A verified Certificate of Origin (CO / Form E / Form R) to obtain preferential customs tariff rates at the destination port.

Q4: How does Weifang Mingze verify the -25°C low-temperature flexibility requirement of EN 13707 in its CNAS laboratory?

Answer: In the Weifang Mingze CNAS-standard testing laboratory, quality technicians condition five longitudinal and five transverse test specimens of MZ-CAT-01 SBS Membrane in a high-precision cryostatic chamber at -25.0°C ± 0.5°C for a minimum of 2 hours. Each specimen is mechanically bent 180° around a Ø 30 mm polished stainless steel mandrel over a standardized 5-second interval per EN 1109. Technicians immediately inspect the top and bottom bituminous coatings under a 6x magnifying glass; the batch passes only if zero surface cracks, tears, or delaminations are detected across all 10 specimens.

Q5: What is the optimal freight and container packing strategy for importing both heavy bitumen membranes and lightweight polymer sheets together?

Answer: Because 4.0mm SBS bitumen membranes (MZ-CAT-01) are weight-limited (reaching the 24.5 metric ton 20GP container payload with only 480–500 rolls occupying 50% container volume) while 1.5mm TPO sheets (MZ-CAT-08) or pre-applied HDPE sheets (MZ-CAT-11) are volume-limited, Weifang Mingze offers custom mixed-container consolidation at Qingdao Port. For instance, loading 250 rolls of 4.0mm bitumen (12.2 tons) alongside 150 rolls of 1.5mm TPO (8.7 tons) achieves 100% weight capacity and 100% volumetric space utilization, optimizing ocean freight economics for regional distributors.
