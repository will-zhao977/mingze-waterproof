Frequently Asked Questions (Technical & Export FAQ)

How to choose between SBS and APP modified bitumen waterproof membranes for different climates?

SBS (Styrene-Butadiene-Styrene) modified bitumen membrane (MZ-CAT-01) offers superior low-temperature flexibility down to -25°C and ≥40% elongation, making it ideal for cold and temperate regions. APP (Atactic Polypropylene) modified bitumen membrane (MZ-CAT-02) provides high heat resistance up to +130°C and strong UV stability, engineered for tropical and high-temperature regions such as the Middle East, Africa, Southeast Asia, and South America.

How many rolls of 3mm or 4mm waterproof membrane fit in a 20GP export container?

For standard 1m × 10m modified bitumen rolls palletized at our Weifang factory: a 20GP container loads approximately 620–650 rolls (6,200–6,500 m²) of 3.0mm SBS/APP membrane, or 480–500 rolls (4,800–5,000 m²) of 4.0mm SBS/APP membrane. For 1.2mm–1.5mm Pre-Applied HDPE or TPO membranes (2m × 20m rolls), a 20GP container loads approximately 14,000–18,000 m². Standard lead time to Qingdao Port (200 km) is 7–10 days.

Which waterproofing membrane is recommended for blindside underground basements and tunnels?

For underground foundations, basements, and civil tunnels, we recommend Pre-Applied HDPE Self-Adhesive Waterproof Membrane (MZ-CAT-11, 1.2mm–2.0mm) compliant with ASTM D5385 and GB/T 23457, which forms a permanent chemical-mechanical bond with poured structural concrete to prevent lateral water migration, paired with EVA/ECB tunnel sheets (MZ-CAT-16) and HDPE dimple drainage boards (MZ-CAT-14 / MZ-CAT-34).
